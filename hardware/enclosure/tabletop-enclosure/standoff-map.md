# Tabletop Enclosure Standoff Map

Use this as your cheat sheet before drilling holes in that pretty enclosure.

## Teensy + Audio Shield Stack
- **Standoff spacing:** 35 mm × 18 mm rectangle. M3 hardware fits the mounting ears.
- **Height:** 12 mm standoffs clear the underside pins while keeping the USB port flush with the rear panel cutout.
- **Ground lug:** Add a threaded insert near the rear-left standoff to bond the enclosure.

## Control Surface Plate
- Pots line up on a 20 mm vertical pitch, 30 mm horizontal spacing. Confirm with `dice-loop-panel.dxf` before milling.
- Buttons sit 12 mm below the bottom pot row to clear the LED bar diffuser.

## Cable Routing
- Leave a 15 mm channel along the right side for the ribbon harness. Tape it down during assembly to avoid pinches when closing the lid.

Log any alternative standoff positions or different hardware sizes you try so the drill template can evolve.
