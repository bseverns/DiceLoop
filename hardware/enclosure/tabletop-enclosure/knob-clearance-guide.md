# Knob Clearance Guide

The control surface needs breathing room or you'll scrape knuckles mid-performance. Use these measurements as a starting point.

## Vertical Stack
- Pots sit 8 mm below the panel surface when mounted with standard washers.
- Leave 5 mm air gap between knob skirts and the LED bar diffuser to avoid blocking light.

## Horizontal Clearance
- Minimum 14 mm center-to-center spacing for 19 mm knobs. If you upgrade to 22 mm knobs, space them at 18 mm and update the DXF.

## Panel Thickness
- Templates assume a 2 mm aluminum panel. If you go thicker, extend the pot bushings or counterbore the backside. Note the method you choose.
